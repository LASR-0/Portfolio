Requests for laptops, phones and peripherals used to arrive as email and leave
as a spreadsheet row that someone forgot to update. AssetCheckout replaced both.

Staff raise a request against a real asset register. Approvals route to the
right manager, the item is marked out, and the return date is tracked against
the person rather than the ticket.

In production and still moving. The next piece is bulk onboarding kits, so a new
starter is one request instead of six.
